Flower Still Life

A quiet still life of sculpted flowers by a window, modeled, textured, and lit from scratch in Blender and rendered in Cycles.

CourseCS 148, Intro to Computer Graphics and Imaging
ToolsBlender, Cycles
Year2024

Modeling

I sculpted the flowers from modified spheres, shaping and placing each petal by hand, and modeled the flower centers, desk, and window from scratch. The vase is an imported asset, scaled to fit the scene.

Texturing

I UV-unwrapped the petals and hand-painted a gradient from yellow at the base to pink at the tips, then added a procedural surface texture with material nodes. The centers use a displacement map for a pollen-like surface. Wood, wall, and window materials combine image textures, displacement, and node setups.

Camera

Depth of field focuses on the flowers and lets the window and room fall softly out of focus.

Assets: vase from Free3D; sunrise HDRI, wall, and wood textures from Poly Haven.
