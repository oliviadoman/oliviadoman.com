Vellum Grains

Vellum grains pushed around by wind forces, colored by the stress between neighboring particles as they move.

ToolsHoudini, Vellum, POP solvers
Based onEntagma abstract particles tutorial

Approach

Vellum constraints handle how grains interact with each other, while POP solvers add wind and other forces at the particle level. Color comes from a stress attribute: how much the distance between neighboring particles changes as they move.

Started from an Entagma tutorial on abstract particles.
