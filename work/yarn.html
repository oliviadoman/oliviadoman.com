Crocheted Yarn Sim

Real crochet stretches a lot because of its loose, looping structure. This simulation uses Houdini's Vellum solver to reproduce that behavior at the level of individual strands.

ToolsHoudini, Vellum

Approach

I built the fabric from crochet stitch geometry and simulated it with Vellum constraints, drawing on research into stitch-level textiles:

  • Guo, Lin, Narayanan, McCann. Representing Crochet with Stitch Meshes. SIGGRAPH.
  • Kaldor, James, Marschner. Simulating Knitted Cloth at the Yarn Level.
  • Ben Watts. Design Through FX. SIGGRAPH 2018.

Tuning

  • Substeps and constraint iterations tuned for detail and stability.
  • Stretch and bend stiffness calibrated to yarn, so the fabric keeps its shape while it drapes.
  • Damping adjusted so it settles naturally without wobbling.

What I would fix next

On the sphere, stretch stiffness had to be high to keep the fabric from sliding off, which introduced small kinks in the yarn. Bend damping reduced them, but some remain. Occasional stray strands also need parameter tweaks or smoothing after the sim.
